# AffiliateProgram review

## Shared-progress selection

The pushed `skylerblue333/frontendpages` repository now contains the EventPlanner, ExperimentFactory, ABTesting, ABTestingAdvanced, Achievements, ActivityFeed, ActivityTracking, AdminDashboard, AdvancedOrders, and AdvancedSearch upgrades. AffiliateProgram is registered at `/affiliate-program` and currently presents generic Feature cards, unsupported live-data and real-time-update claims, fabricated active-user, transaction, success-rate, and response-time metrics, and unsupported Get Started, Learn More, and Documentation actions.

## Upgrade scope

Replace the generic affiliate surface with a local referral-program preview. Provide typed program fixtures, eligibility-state filtering, selected attribution and policy detail, explicit identity and commission-unavailable labels, and blocked referral or payout feedback. Preserve the affiliate-program concept while making the absence of referral links, identities, conversions, commission ledger, payout provider, tax, and financial services visible.

## Safety boundaries

No referral link is generated, click is tracked, person is identified, conversion is attributed, commission is calculated, payout is initiated, tax status is inferred, or financial metric is fabricated. No identity, contact, campaign, cookie, device, payment, balance, or account state is accessed. No program, referral, attribution, notification, payout, or external integration operation occurs. Future affiliate functionality requires consent, fraud controls, attribution windows, duplicate protection, payout reconciliation, tax handling, privacy filtering, and immutable auditability.
