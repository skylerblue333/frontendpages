# DepartmentManagement review

The route `/department-management` is a generic authenticated-only placeholder with no organization directory, department registry, member source, role and permission service, reporting structure, or audit contract.

The hardening target is a truthful local department-organization preview with typed department concepts, area and state filters, explicit member, owner, permission, reporting, and audit unavailable states, and blocked create/manage actions. No department, member, staffing count, permission, organizational relationship, or admin outcome will be fabricated. Production management requires authorization, least privilege, privacy-aware directory handling, audit trails, and server-side mutation controls.
