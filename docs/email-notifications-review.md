# EmailNotifications review

The former route was a generic unavailable placeholder. It has been replaced with a typed notification-readiness workspace that makes no claim that an account preference, event source, provider, recipient, template, message, delivery result, or subscription record is connected.

The searchable requirement map covers event routing and consent, delivery and suppression, quiet hours and privacy, and audit and recovery. Search and manage/refresh controls only filter static notes or update an in-page live status. They never inspect or mutate preferences, recipients, providers, messages, or delivery data.

`prettier`, `tsc --noEmit`, and the production build completed successfully. Desktop and mobile evidence show the notification-service boundary, requirement map, unavailable controls, and activation evidence requirements at 1440×1000 and 390×844. No notification preference, message, delivery, subscription, monitoring, or rollback result is fabricated.
