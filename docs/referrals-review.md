# Referrals review

The `/referrals` route currently renders mock names, email addresses, dates, statuses, earnings, commission language, and copy/share actions with success feedback. These are personal, social, and financial claims requiring authenticated identity, consent, attribution, fraud controls, payout rules, privacy, and durable referral records.

The safe replacement is a strictly typed local referral-readiness view. Preserve the referral explanation and local draft link structure, but mark identity, email, referral attribution, status, earnings, commission, copy, share, and payout outcomes unavailable. No contact detail should be collected or transmitted.
