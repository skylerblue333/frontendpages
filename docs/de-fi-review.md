# DeFi review

The former route displayed an unavailable notice describing hard-coded protocols, TVL, APY, volume, risk ratings, liquidity pools, lending, bridge support, and earning actions that must not be presented as live. It has been replaced with a strictly typed, accessible, local-only decentralized-finance readiness workspace.

The new screen states that no network, protocol, contract, market, wallet, balance, position, transaction, yield, or saved DeFi record is loaded or persisted. Protocol loading, quote review, wallet connection, and transaction execution controls are disabled. The route documents network and chain identity, RPCs, protocols, contracts, ABIs, deployment provenance, upgrades, provider health, token pairs, prices, TVL, APY, volume, pools, reserves, oracles, collateral, borrowing, liquidation, fees, utilization, wallets, accounts, allowances, signatures, quotes, slippage, gas, MEV, simulation, status, receipts, settlement, custody, monitoring, incidents, bridge recovery, reconciliation, audit, sanctions, and non-advice boundaries. Its search field filters static capability notes only and never queries chains, reads balances, shows prices or yield, accesses wallets, calculates returns, or executes an on-chain action.

`prettier`, `tsc --noEmit`, and the production build completed successfully. Desktop evidence shows balanced DeFi-state/release cards and a four-item capability map. Mobile evidence stacks the same content cleanly at 390×844 without clipping; chain, market, wallet, execution, custody, risk, monitoring, reconciliation, audit, and unavailable-action disclosures remain readable. No chain, contract, price, liquidity, yield, balance, transaction, or financial outcome is fabricated.
