# EnvironmentManagement review

The former route was an authenticated CRUD shell with sign-in, new-item, search, settings, loading, and empty-state controls but no verified environment contract. It has been replaced with a typed environment-readiness workspace that makes no claim that development, staging, production, region, cluster, cloud account, deployment target, secret store, operator role, health signal, or incident system is connected.

The screen explicitly shows no deployment actions, no secrets exposed, and no operational signals. Its searchable readiness map covers environment identity, secrets and configuration, deploy and rollback, and access and observability. Search and manage controls only filter static boundary notes or update an in-page status; they never inspect infrastructure, credentials, deployments, logs, or monitoring storage, and they cannot mutate systems.

`prettier`, `tsc --noEmit`, and the production build completed successfully. Desktop and mobile evidence show the unavailable control plane, truthful no-operation cards, readiness map, and activation evidence requirements at 1440×1000 and 390×844. No environment, credential, deployment, rollback, log, metric, trace, alert, incident, or health result is fabricated.
