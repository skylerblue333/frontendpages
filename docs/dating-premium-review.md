# DatingPremium review

The `/dating-premium` route presents hard-coded subscription prices and entitlements, profile boost and super-like purchases, AI profile optimization, priority and trust features, and unsupported claims such as ten-times visibility and three-times higher match likelihood. Upgrade and purchase actions currently show success toasts without checkout, billing, entitlement, receipt, refund, or service activation.

The safe replacement must not imply prices are current, that a subscription is active, that a purchase succeeded, or that premium features produce measured outcomes. It should present a billing-readiness preview, clearly label any plan concepts as unpriced/unavailable, block checkout and purchase actions, and state that no payment or entitlement mutation occurs.
