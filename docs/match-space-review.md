# MatchSpace review

The `/match-space` route currently renders named profiles, ages, roles, distances, online state, relationship graph positions, compatibility scores, trust and emotional scores, AI matching reasons, and like/pass/super-like actions. These values imply sensitive personal data, location, behavioral profiling, relationship recommendations, and social outcomes that are not backed by verified identity, consent, safety, moderation, matching, or messaging services.

The safe replacement must use clearly labeled local match concepts rather than real-looking people or scores. It should not infer trust, emotion, behavior, distance, online presence, or compatibility. Like, pass, super-like, messaging, and matching actions must be no-ops with explicit unavailable status. A graph can remain as an abstract relationship-model illustration only.
