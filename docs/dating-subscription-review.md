# DatingSubscription review

The former route displayed an unavailable billing notice, but it did not provide a structured readiness contract. It has been replaced with a strictly typed, accessible, local-only subscription-readiness workspace.

The new screen states that no account, plan, price, entitlement, payment, subscription, renewal, invoice, or saved billing record is loaded or persisted. Plan loading, checkout, plan change, and cancellation controls are disabled. The route documents products, plans, currencies, prices, trials, renewals, entitlements, feature gates, account scope, catalog versions, customers, payment methods, provider tokens, authorization, checkout, charges, declines, receipts, subscription lifecycle, activations, pauses, cancellations, upgrades, downgrades, webhook signatures, retries, idempotency, refunds, proration, taxes, invoices, retention, consent, notifications, reconciliation, and billing audit events. Its search field filters static capability notes only and never queries plans, shows prices, accesses payment methods, starts checkout, changes entitlements, sends notifications, or persists billing state.

`prettier`, `tsc --noEmit`, and the production build completed successfully. Desktop evidence shows balanced subscription-state/release cards and a four-item capability map. Mobile evidence stacks the same content cleanly at 390×844 without clipping; plan, payment, lifecycle, refund, tax, privacy, audit, and unavailable-action disclosures remain readable. No price, payment method, subscription, renewal, entitlement, invoice, refund, or billing outcome is fabricated.
