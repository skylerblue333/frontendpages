# SMTPSettings review

The `/s-m-t-p-settings` route already contained a substantial local email connectivity-governance preview, so the implementation was preserved rather than rewritten. It now has formatting, strict typecheck/build validation, and desktop/mobile evidence documentation. The route is not treated as a live SMTP provider connection, credential store, sender registry, mailbox, delivery report, bounce processor, or compliance certification.

| Area | Result |
|---|---|
| Local settings functionality | Transactional mail provider, sender identity, delivery/bounce webhook, and email privacy concepts, search, category filters, selected-setting state, transport and credential boundary intent, save state, reset, email-gate toggling, and disabled connect/verify/test-send/rotate controls remain interactive in local component state. |
| Evidence boundary | The hero and evidence banner explicitly state that this is a local SMTP-governance preview, not evidence that an email provider, secret, sender, recipient, mailbox, or delivery event exists. No host, username, password, API key, mailbox, email, delivery, bounce, spam, cost, or compliance outcome is asserted. |
| Secret and transport safety | Real activation requires server-side provider credentials, sender identity, domain authentication, tenant, purpose, secret rotation, environment separation, TLS, certificate validation, least privilege, timeout, retry, idempotency, and failure handling. No secret is entered, stored, exposed, or transmitted by this preview. |
| Delivery and suppression safety | Production email requires delivery, bounce, complaint, unsubscribe, suppression, cost, provider status, callbacks, support, monitoring, recovery, and signed/replay-protected processing. No provider, callback, mailbox, delivery, bounce, complaint, or suppression state is connected. |
| Privacy and communications safety | Recipient provenance, consent, purpose, privacy, sensitive-data handling, retention, deletion, export, lawful communications, and audit must be governed before connection. No recipient, email address, consent, or personal-data record is connected. |
| Persistence and actions | Save and reset operate only on local settings state. Connect, verify, send test, and rotate remain visibly disabled. No provider, credential, sender, mailbox, message, delivery, bounce, cost, compliance, or audit record is created. |
| Accessibility and UX | Existing semantic controls, visible labels, focusable buttons, native selects, responsive cards, disabled-state treatment, and evidence disclosures were retained. Desktop and mobile visual hierarchy was reviewed. |

Validation completed successfully with Prettier, `pnpm exec tsc --noEmit`, and the production build. The build continues to emit the existing non-blocking large-chunk advisory. Desktop evidence was captured at 1440×1000 and mobile evidence at 390×844. Visual review confirms preserved local settings behavior, explicit unavailable/unconfigured/blocked states, disabled consequential actions, and absence of fabricated outcomes.

Production activation would require server-side secret handling, provider configuration, authenticated sender and domain, TLS, signed callbacks, idempotent delivery and bounce processing, suppression and unsubscribe enforcement, privacy and retention controls, rate limits, monitoring, audit, support, incident response, and accountable domain review for transactional, marketing, education, community, financial, security, regulated, or user-impact communications. No email send, delivery, bounce, cost, compliance, or business outcome is claimed here.
