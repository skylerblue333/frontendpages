# DAOTreasury review

The former route presented an authenticated-only generic treasury-management shell with a misleading New action, settings action, empty-state creation prompt, unused loading state, and no trustworthy asset, signer, proposal, or transaction contract. It has been replaced with a strictly typed, accessible, local-only DAO treasury-governance readiness workspace.

The new screen states that no DAO, wallet, signer, asset, balance, valuation, proposal, vote, transfer, or saved treasury record is loaded or persisted. Treasury loading, proposal creation, vote review, and transfer authorization controls are disabled. The route documents DAO identity, wallets, signers, networks, assets, balances, valuation, custody policy, ledgers, reconciliation, proposals, proposers, agendas, quorum, voting power, delegation, eligibility, votes, thresholds, final decisions, recipients, amounts, allowances, multisig approvals, timelocks, transfers, failures, cancellations, settlement, permissions, segregation of duties, limits, sanctions, risk review, statements, incidents, and audit. Its search field filters static capability notes only and never queries wallets, reads balances, creates proposals, counts votes, authorizes transfers, or persists a ledger event.

`prettier`, `tsc --noEmit`, and the production build completed successfully. Desktop evidence shows balanced treasury-state/release cards and a four-item capability map. Mobile evidence stacks the same content cleanly at 390×844 without clipping; custody, governance, voting, transfer, risk, reporting, audit, and unavailable-action disclosures remain readable. No custody, balance, proposal, vote, transfer, settlement, or financial outcome is fabricated.
