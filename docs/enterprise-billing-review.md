# EnterpriseBilling review

The former route was an unavailable billing placeholder. It has been replaced with a typed billing-readiness workspace that makes no claim that a billing provider, customer identity, organization owner, plan, invoice, balance, currency, tax result, payment method, checkout session, charge, refund, or webhook event is connected.

The screen explicitly shows no financial records, no payment actions, and no reconciliation state. Its searchable readiness map covers provider and account ownership, plans/invoices/tax, payment methods and changes, and webhook reconciliation. Search and manage controls only filter static boundary notes or update an in-page status; they never inspect financial storage, create a charge, mutate a subscription, issue a refund, or initiate checkout.

`prettier`, `tsc --noEmit`, and the production build completed successfully. Desktop and mobile evidence show the unavailable provider, truthful no-payment cards, readiness map, and activation evidence requirements at 1440×1000 and 390×844. No plan, invoice, balance, amount, currency, tax, receipt, payment status, charge, payout, refund, or webhook result is fabricated.
