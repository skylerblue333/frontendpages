| Area | Result |
|---|---|
| Source, sampling, and freshness | No market source, asset, instrument, language, sample, collection timestamp, freshness policy, license, or provenance record is connected. |
| Sentiment method and calibration | No taxonomy, label definition, classifier, model version, scoring scale, confidence calibration, baseline, or annotation policy is configured. |
| Bias, uncertainty, and comparability | No bot filtering, duplicate handling, language bias review, subgroup analysis, uncertainty interval, period, or comparable cohort exists. |
| Privacy and market-data governance | No lawful collection, personal-data minimization, sensitive-source rule, retention, redaction, export, correction, or publication approval is verified. |
| Monitoring and decision boundary | No drift signal, source outage, quality alert, analyst review, recommendation policy, audit event, rollback, or recovery evidence is available. |
| Interaction boundary | Search filters immutable local notes; review buttons update only an `aria-live` unavailable status. No source, sample, sentiment score, trend, alert, signal, recommendation, or market-data mutation is created. |
| Accessibility | Semantic landmarks, labelled search, keyboard-safe buttons, category badges, decorative icon hiding, and live status feedback are included. |

Validation completed successfully with Prettier, `pnpm exec tsc --noEmit`, and the production build. The build continues to emit the existing non-blocking large-chunk advisory. Desktop evidence was captured at 1440×1000 and mobile evidence at 390×844. Visual review confirms the sentiment-service-unavailable boundary, no-sentiment-data/no-verified-signals/no-sentiment-actions disclosures, governance map, and responsive hierarchy without fabricated market research.

Production activation requires licensed and fresh sources, representative sampling, documented labels and methods, calibrated uncertainty, bot and bias handling, privacy controls, analyst review, correction and provenance, monitored quality, and a clear boundary against unsupported trading or financial recommendations. No sentiment score or market conclusion is claimed here.
