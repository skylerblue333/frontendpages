# About review

The `/about` page is a custom marketing and founder-story surface, not a generic placeholder. Its visual hierarchy, founder imagery, feature cards, timeline, and CTA should be preserved rather than rewritten. The audit identified multiple unsupported production claims that must not be presented as verified platform facts: `48,200+ Active Users`, `2.4M+ SKY444 Distributed`, `$127K+ Charities Funded`, `890K+ Games Played`, browser mining, on-chain certificates, 44 deployed AI agents, real-time market intelligence, and `48,200+ users already earning`.

Safe hardening is limited to truthful disclosure and accessibility/maintainability improvements. Replace unverified metrics and capability claims with clearly labeled local preview or unavailable states unless authoritative services and evidence exist; avoid implying cryptocurrency earnings, mining success, charity verification, AI deployment, token listing, DeFi availability, ICO launch, or production adoption. Preserve CTA routes but ensure they do not imply a guaranteed account, mining, financial, or reward outcome. The page should include descriptive image alt text, keyboard-accessible links, non-color-only status cues, and a concise platform-evidence disclaimer.
