# EventAnalytics review

The former route was an authenticated CRUD shell with unused data, auth, loading, settings, and new-item controls while claiming attendance tracking. It has been replaced with a typed event-analytics readiness workspace that makes no claim that event records, attendees, registrations, check-ins, consent, attribution, funnels, cohorts, or analytics queries are connected.

The screen explicitly shows no attendee data, no cohort values, and no analytics output. Its searchable readiness map covers event and attendance identity, funnel and cohort logic, privacy and retention, and exports and operational data. Search and manage controls only filter static boundary notes or update an in-page status; they never inspect events, attendees, cohorts, counts, charts, or analytics storage, and they cannot mutate systems.

`prettier`, `tsc --noEmit`, and the production build completed successfully. Desktop and mobile evidence show the unavailable event source, truthful no-operation cards, readiness map, and activation evidence requirements at 1440×1000 and 390×844. No event, attendee, registration, check-in, consent, count, trend, chart, report, export, alert, or query result is fabricated.
