# ContactManagement review

The pushed `skylerblue333/frontendpages` repository is clean and synchronized at `fff5c28`. ContactManagement is registered at `/contact-management` and currently presents an authenticated-only generic search and empty-state screen that implies contact management without a connected identity directory or privacy-aware contact service.

The upgrade will replace it with a local contact-concept preview using typed contact categories and states, search and filters, selected-contact details, explicit identity/consent/channel/last-contact/owner unavailable fields, and blocked edit, message, and create actions.

No personal identity, email, phone number, relationship, communication history, consent, notification, or contact record will be fabricated or queried. Production contact management requires verified identity, purpose limitation, privacy controls, consent, access authorization, retention rules, and auditable data changes.
