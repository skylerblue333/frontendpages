# Sponsorships review

The `/sponsorships` route was upgraded from a generic unavailable placeholder into a local evidence-bounded partnership-planning workspace without connecting sponsor or recipient identity, agreement terms, deliverables, disclosure obligations, scheduling, funding authorization, payment, attribution, privacy, tax, support, or audit systems. It preserves sponsorship concepts, search and filter intent, agreement/schedule intent, selected concept detail, local save/reset behavior, disclosures, and disabled consequential actions.

| Area | Result |
|---|---|
| Data boundary | No sponsor, recipient, agreement, funding, payment, deliverable, conversion, revenue, or financial outcome is asserted. |
| Safety | Real activation requires verified parties, written terms, deliverable and disclosure review, payment and tax controls, consent, privacy, attribution, moderation, cancellation, support, reconciliation, and audit. |
| Mutations | Search, sponsorship selection, agreement/schedule intent, save, and reset are local-only. Propose, approve, fund, publish, and report remain disabled. |
| Validation | Prettier, strict TypeScript, production build, and desktop/mobile captures completed. The existing large-chunk advisory remains non-blocking. |

The route is not a sponsorship marketplace, agreement authority, payment/funding service, campaign-delivery system, tax service, attribution report, or financial-outcome predictor.
