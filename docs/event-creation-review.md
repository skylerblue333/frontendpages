# EventCreation review

The former route was an authenticated CRUD shell with unused data, auth, loading, settings, and new-item controls while claiming that new events could be created. It has been replaced with a typed event-creation readiness workspace that makes no claim that an organizer, event store, venue, capacity system, attendee directory, reminder channel, moderation queue, or publication workflow is connected.

The screen explicitly shows no draft persisted, no people notified, and no publication action. Its searchable readiness map covers identity and ownership, schedule and capacity, people and accessibility, and publication and lifecycle. Search and manage controls only filter static boundary notes or update an in-page status; they never inspect event storage, attendees, invitations, reminders, or publication systems, and they cannot create, publish, edit, cancel, sync, audit, or delete an event.

`prettier`, `tsc --noEmit`, and the production build completed successfully. Desktop and mobile evidence show the unavailable event service, truthful no-operation cards, readiness map, and activation evidence requirements at 1440×1000 and 390×844. No draft, event, attendee, invitation, consent, accessibility, reminder, moderation, approval, or publication result is fabricated.
