# ProductBrain review

The `/product-brain` route was upgraded from a generic placeholder into a **product-knowledge readiness workspace**. It does not claim that source documents, playbooks, insights, usage counts, adoption, retention, revenue, effectiveness scores, recommendations, analytics records, or knowledge-base entries exist.

| Area | Result |
|---|---|
| Source documents and provenance | No product brief, research note, support record, experiment result, source URL, author, timestamp, or claim provenance is connected. |
| Ownership, access, and versioning | No document owner, reviewer, role, permission, workspace boundary, version history, approval, or change audit exists. |
| Evaluation and recommendation method | No playbook, insight, rubric, sample, metric definition, confidence, effectiveness score, recommendation, or human review is verified. |
| Analytics reconciliation and privacy | No usage, adoption, retention, revenue, customer, or behavioral analytics source is connected or reconciled; no privacy boundary is established. |
| Actions and persistence | No import, index, annotate, publish, approve, archive, recommend, export, or product-knowledge mutation is connected or persisted. |
| Interaction boundary | Search filters immutable local requirement notes; review buttons update only an `aria-live` unavailable status. No source, insight, analytics, recommendation, or knowledge record is created. |
| Accessibility | Semantic main and section structure, labelled search, keyboard-safe buttons, badges, decorative icon hiding, and live status feedback are included. |

The interface prominently states that Product Brain is unavailable and cannot import, index, annotate, publish, approve, archive, recommend, export, or claim product insights. It retains a useful governance surface without fabricating product intelligence or analytics.

Validation completed successfully with Prettier, `pnpm exec tsc --noEmit`, and the production build. The build continues to emit the existing non-blocking large-chunk advisory. Desktop evidence was captured at 1440×1000 and mobile evidence at 390×844. Visual review confirms the unavailable-knowledge boundary, no-knowledge-state/no-analytics-claims/no-knowledge-actions disclosures, governance requirements map, and responsive hierarchy.

Production product intelligence requires sourced documents, claim provenance, ownership, access and privacy controls, version and approval history, evaluation methodology, analytics definitions and reconciliation, confidence and uncertainty, human review, and safe publication. Unsupported ecosystem, mining, finance, fundraising, certification, enterprise, deployment, product, customer, revenue, retention, adoption, or recommendation claims must remain undisclosed until evidenced. No playbook, insight, usage, effectiveness, recommendation, revenue, retention, or adoption record is claimed here.
