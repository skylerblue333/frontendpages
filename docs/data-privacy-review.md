# DataPrivacy review

The pushed `skylerblue333/frontendpages` repository is clean and synchronized at `43d0a4e`. The upgrade will replace it with a local privacy-controls preview using typed privacy-control concepts, control-area and state filters, selected-control details, explicit legal basis, scope, consent, retention, access, and audit unavailable fields, and blocked change/request actions.

No consent decision, legal conclusion, jurisdiction, personal-data inventory, retention period, identity verification, data-subject request, privacy preference, or compliance status will be fabricated or queried.
