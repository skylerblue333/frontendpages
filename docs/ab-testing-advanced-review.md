No audience segment, cookie, device identifier, event telemetry, conversion, confidence, sample size, guardrail, revenue, recommendation, rollout, or production decision is accessed or fabricated. No advanced test is created, started, paused, concluded, persisted, or exported. Future advanced experimentation requires consent and privacy controls, deterministic assignment, metric definitions, guardrails, statistical review, rollback, least privilege, and auditable approvals.
