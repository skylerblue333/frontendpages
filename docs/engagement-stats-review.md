# EngagementStats review

The former route was a generic unavailable placeholder. It has been replaced with a typed engagement-statistics readiness workspace that makes no claim that aggregate values, trends, cohorts, denominators, reporting periods, timezones, charts, or exports are connected.

The screen explicitly shows no trend values, no cohort values, and no reporting period. Its searchable readiness map covers trend and aggregate data, cohort and denominator rules, time range and timezone, and privacy and export safety. Search and manage controls only filter static boundary notes or update an in-page status; they never inspect events, users, cohorts, values, charts, or analytics storage.

`prettier`, `tsc --noEmit`, and the production build completed successfully. Desktop and mobile evidence show the unavailable statistics source, truthful zero-value cards, readiness map, and activation evidence requirements at 1440×1000 and 390×844. No count, percentage, change, average, trend, cohort, chart point, export, or user activity is fabricated.
