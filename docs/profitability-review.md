# Profitability review

The `/profitability` route was upgraded from a generic placeholder that presented unsupported financial claims into a **finance-safe profitability readiness workspace**. It does not claim that revenue, costs, margins, fees, balances, forecasts, projections, business-model economics, targets, payments, orders, taxes, or financial records exist.

| Area | Result |
|---|---|
| Source-backed financial records | No ledger, invoice, order, payment, fee, subscription, marketplace, creator, API, advertising, charity, staking, or AI-credit record is connected. |
| Accounting methodology and period discipline | No accounting basis, fiscal period, recognition policy, currency, unit, consolidation scope, adjustment, or metric definition is verified. |
| Reconciliation, tax, and privacy | No bank, processor, order, wallet, tax, refund, chargeback, or personal-data reconciliation, tax treatment, privacy boundary, or audit trail exists. |
| Forecasts, assumptions, and approvals | No forecast period, assumption, growth rate, target, scenario, sensitivity, owner approval, independent review, or confidence assessment is connected. |
| Actions and persistence | No import, calculate, reconcile, approve, publish, export, revise, or revenue, profitability, projection, or financial-record mutation is connected or persisted. |
| Interaction boundary | Search filters immutable local requirement notes; review buttons update only an `aria-live` unavailable status. No financial, forecast, tax, payment, order, or personal-data record is created. |
| Accessibility | Semantic main and section structure, labelled search, keyboard-safe buttons, badges, decorative icon hiding, and live status feedback are included. |

The interface prominently states that profitability reporting is unavailable and cannot import, calculate, reconcile, approve, publish, export, revise, or claim revenue, profitability, projections, business-model economics, or financial targets. It retains a useful governance surface without fabricating financial information.

Validation completed successfully with Prettier, `pnpm exec tsc --noEmit`, and the production build. The build continues to emit the existing non-blocking large-chunk advisory. Desktop evidence was captured at 1440×1000 and mobile evidence at 390×844. Visual review confirms the unavailable-financial boundary, no-financial-records/no-profitability-metrics/no-reporting-actions disclosures, governance requirements map, and responsive hierarchy.

Production profitability reporting requires source-backed ledgers and transaction records, fiscal-period and accounting-basis discipline, currency and unit controls, payment and order reconciliation, tax and privacy treatment, forecast assumptions and scenario definitions, owner approval, independent review, and auditable correction history. Unsupported ecosystem, mining, finance, fundraising, certification, enterprise, deployment, revenue, fee, margin, balance, forecast, projection, target, or financial-data claims must remain undisclosed until evidenced. No revenue, fee, cost, margin, forecast, projection, target, balance, or financial-data record is claimed here.
