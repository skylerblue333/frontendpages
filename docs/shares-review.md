# Shares review

The `/shares` route was upgraded into a local evidence-bounded ownership-design workspace without connecting issuers, holder registries, legal structures, market feeds, valuation, settlement, custody, accounting, tax, risk, or audit systems. It preserves governance, marketplace, creator, and test concepts, filtering, selected-concept detail, illustrative portfolio framing, local watchlist/reset behavior, disclosures, and disabled consequential actions.

| Area | Result |
|---|---|
| Data boundary | No ownership, balance, market data, price, valuation, liquidity, dividend, return, issuer, legal status, investment outcome, or financial advice is asserted. |
| Safety | Real activation requires issuer and legal structure, jurisdiction and security classification, holder registry, ownership rights, transfer/voting rules, disclosures, authenticated portfolio and price provenance, valuation methodology, market depth, settlement, custody, reconciliation, tax, risk, and qualified review. |
| Mutations | Watchlist save and reset are local-only. Buy, sell, transfer, and claim-dividend remain disabled. |
| Validation | Prettier, strict TypeScript, production build, and desktop/mobile captures completed. The existing large-chunk advisory remains non-blocking. |

The route is not an investment product, security, ownership registry, marketplace, market-data feed, portfolio, dividend system, or financial-advice surface.
