# CreateArticle review

The `/create-article` route provides a useful local editor structure, but its draft, preview, visibility, tags, publish/save behavior, authorship, subscriber access, and monetization language require verified identity, content persistence, moderation, entitlement, payment, tax, and payout services.

The safe upgrade should preserve local drafting and preview while explicitly stating that drafts are local only, publishing and saving are unavailable, visibility is a preview setting rather than a live access policy, and subscriber/revenue/token claims remain unavailable until verified services are connected.
