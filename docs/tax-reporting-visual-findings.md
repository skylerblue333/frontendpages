# TaxReporting visual findings

The 390×844 mobile render keeps the tax-reporting header, tax-specific unavailable disclosure, reset control, reporting-concepts preview, and first reporting card readable without horizontal overflow. The 1440×1000 desktop render shows the complete reporting workspace with individual, business, and digital-asset concepts, selected-report metadata, jurisdiction/source-ledger/cost-basis/liability/filing-status boundaries, and withheld actions. The interface explicitly states that it is not tax advice or a filing and directs consequential verification to a CPA or tax professional. No tax return, tax period, jurisdiction, taxpayer identity, income, gain, loss, cost basis, deduction, credit, liability, filing deadline, compliance state, payment, refund, or submission outcome is fabricated. Calculate, export, submit, and reset interactions remain local-only or unavailable.
