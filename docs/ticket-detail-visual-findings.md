# TicketDetail visual findings

The first mobile review exposed an overly long hero title and badge that crowded the 390px layout. The route was corrected with a shorter title, concise description, and contained `Ticket detail preview` badge, then re-captured and re-reviewed. The corrected 390×844 render is readable, with the hero controls and summary cards stacking cleanly; the 1440×1000 render preserves balanced hero composition, evidence boundary, support-state cards, local detail controls, and selected-ticket metadata. Both captures explicitly disclose that no ticket record, requester or agent identity, message, attachment, routing, SLA, response, escalation, refund, resolution, or customer outcome is connected. Save, category/severity selection, gates, and reset remain local-only; reply, attach, escalate, and close actions remain unavailable.
