# SkySchoolAI review

The `/sky-school-a-i` route was upgraded from a generic unavailable screen into a local evidence-bounded AI-learning workspace without connecting model providers, prompts, curriculum, instructors, learners, enrollment, progress, assessment, certification, pricing, rewards, privacy, accessibility, safeguarding, or audit systems. It preserves local AI-learning concepts, search/category filtering, selected-concept detail, learning-path and assessment intent, model/provider boundaries, local save/reset behavior, disclosures, and disabled consequential actions.

| Area | Result |
|---|---|
| Data boundary | No model response, AI teaching capability, curriculum, instructor, learner, enrollment, progress, grade, credential, employment, earnings, or certificate outcome is asserted. |
| Safety | Real activation requires approved model/provider governance, prompt and data controls, curriculum provenance, instructor authorization, learner privacy, safety review, accessibility, safeguarding, assessment and appeals, progress persistence, certificate rules, and audit. |
| Mutations | Search, category selection, learning-path and assessment intent, save, and reset are local-only. Enroll, start, submit, and certificate actions remain disabled. |
| Validation | Prettier, strict TypeScript, production build, and desktop/mobile captures completed. The existing large-chunk advisory remains non-blocking. |

The route is not an AI tutor, course catalog, model endpoint, enrollment system, gradebook, certificate issuer, reward ledger, or educational-outcome service.
