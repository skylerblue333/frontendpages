# EngagementMetrics review

The former route was an authenticated CRUD shell with unused data, auth, loading, settings, and new-item controls. It has been replaced with a typed engagement-metrics readiness workspace that makes no claim that event, identity, session, consent, cohort, attribution, reporting, or analytics data is connected.

The searchable readiness map covers event and identity data, time range and calculation, privacy and access, and export and operations. Search and manage controls only filter static boundary notes or update an in-page status. They never inspect events, users, cohorts, scores, counts, exports, or analytics storage. No live dashboard, metric result, or mutation is presented.

`prettier`, `tsc --noEmit`, and the production build completed successfully. Desktop and mobile evidence show the unavailable analytics source, readiness map, safe controls, and activation evidence requirements at 1440×1000 and 390×844. No event, identity, score, count, cohort, export, alert, or user-activity result is fabricated.
