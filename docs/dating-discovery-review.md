# DatingDiscovery review

The `/dating-discovery` route fetches recommended profiles from `/api/dating/discover`, renders profile images, names, ages, locations, biographies, interests, and compatibility percentages, then POSTs like, super-like, and pass actions to `/api/dating/like`. It also reports local like/super-like/viewed counts as if they were meaningful discovery outcomes.

Until consent, age assurance, profile authorization, safety moderation, recommendation provenance, privacy controls, and durable interaction APIs are verified, these are unsafe identity, location, profiling, and social-mutation claims. The safe replacement should show abstract discovery requirements, keep any filters local, and make refresh, like, pass, super-like, profile opening, and recommendation actions explicit no-ops.
