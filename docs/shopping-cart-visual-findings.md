# ShoppingCart visual findings

The 390×844 mobile render preserves the evidence-bounded commerce hero, explicit no-catalog/no-stock/no-price/no-tax/no-payment/no-order/no-fulfillment/no-refund/no-customer-outcome disclosure, local save/gates/reset controls, and stacked cart summary without horizontal overflow. The 1440×1000 desktop render shows the full commerce workspace with gradient hero, commerce workspace badge, items/inventory/subtotal/checkout cards, commerce evidence boundary, product-concept search, and responsive hierarchy. Both captures disclose that catalog ownership, inventory, currency, price, tax, shipping, payment, order, fulfillment, returns, refunds, privacy, and audit are unavailable or unconnected. Checkout, coupon, tax-estimate, and place-order actions remain disabled; no fabricated product, stock, price, discount, tax, payment authorization, order, shipment, refund, or customer outcome is present.
