# ExperimentFactory review

## Shared-progress selection

The pushed `skylerblue333/frontendpages` repository now contains the EventPlanner upgrade. ExperimentFactory is registered at `/experiment-factory` and currently presents fabricated experiment results, confidence, sample sizes, recommendations, deployment claims, automated insights, and untyped metric iteration.

## Upgrade scope

Replace unsupported analytics claims with a local experiment-planning preview. Provide typed experiment fixtures, lifecycle filtering, selected hypothesis and variant detail, explicit metric-unavailable labels, and blocked execution feedback. Preserve the product concept while making the absence of assignment, telemetry, analysis, and rollout infrastructure visible.

## Safety boundaries

No user is assigned, bucketed, tracked, profiled, messaged, or exposed to a variant. No event telemetry, conversion, confidence, sample size, revenue, recommendation, lift, success rate, rollout, deployment, or production decision is accessed or fabricated. No experiment is started, paused, concluded, exported, or persisted. Future experimentation requires consent and privacy controls, deterministic assignment, metric definitions, statistical review, guardrails, rollback, tenant isolation, rate limits, and auditable approvals.
