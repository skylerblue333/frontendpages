# DerivativeTrading review

The former route was a generic unavailable placeholder. It has been replaced with a strictly typed, accessible, local-only derivatives-trading readiness workspace.

The new screen states that no instrument, market, account, margin, leverage, order, position, liquidation, transaction, or saved trading record is loaded or persisted. Instrument loading, margin review, order preview, and trade placement controls are disabled. The route documents underlying assets, contracts, venues, indexes, oracles, price feeds, marks, funding, expiry, liquidity, market hours, collateral, initial and maintenance margin, leverage, position modes, liquidation, insurance, risk engines, accounts, suitability, jurisdiction, orders, quotes, approvals, signatures, slippage, fees, fills, positions, settlement, custody, loss disclosures, sanctions, monitoring, reconciliation, incidents, notifications, audit, and non-advice boundaries. Its search field filters static capability notes only and never queries instruments or prices, calculates margin or leverage, accesses an account, previews an order, or executes a trade.

`prettier`, `tsc --noEmit`, and the production build completed successfully. Desktop evidence shows balanced derivatives-state/release cards and a four-item capability map. Mobile evidence stacks the same content cleanly at 390×844 without clipping; instrument, margin, leverage, liquidation, execution, custody, compliance, audit, and unavailable-action disclosures remain readable. No instrument, price, margin, leverage, position, fill, liquidation, or financial outcome is fabricated.
