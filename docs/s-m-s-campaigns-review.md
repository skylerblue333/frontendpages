# SMSCampaigns review

The `/s-m-s-campaigns` route already contained a substantial local messaging-governance preview, so the implementation was preserved rather than rewritten. It now has formatting, strict typecheck/build validation, and desktop/mobile evidence documentation. The route is not treated as a live SMS provider, recipient directory, consent ledger, delivery report, marketing analytics system, or compliance certification.

| Area | Result |
|---|---|
| Local campaign functionality | Lifecycle, transactional, education, and community SMS campaign concepts, search, category filters, selected-campaign state, audience and consent intent, save state, reset, messaging-gate toggling, and disabled send/schedule/test/export controls remain interactive in local component state. |
| Evidence boundary | The hero and evidence banner explicitly state that this is a local messaging-governance preview, not evidence that recipients, phone numbers, consent, delivery, or campaign results exist. No person, phone number, subscription, message, delivery, open, click, conversion, cost, or compliance outcome is asserted. |
| Consent and recipient safety | Real activation requires authenticated recipient and phone provenance, tenant, consent, purpose, timestamps, locale, opt-out history, transactional exception review, privacy, safeguarding, deletion, lawful communications, and accountable approval. No recipient, phone, consent, or user record is connected. |
| Delivery and provider safety | Production sending requires governed templates, sender identity, character limits, localization, accessibility, quiet hours, provider identity, delivery and retry status, failure handling, idempotency, rate limits, cost, callbacks, and support ownership. No provider or delivery state is connected. |
| Education, security, and abuse controls | Learning reminders require learner consent, safeguarding, schedule, content authority, accessibility, and privacy. Security notifications require event provenance, identity, urgency policy, delivery status, retry, accessibility, and support. Community messaging requires moderator approval, audience boundaries, anti-abuse controls, language support, and appeals. |
| Persistence and actions | Save and reset operate only on local campaign state. Send, schedule, test, and export remain visibly disabled. No recipient, message, provider, delivery, opt-out, campaign, marketing, cost, or compliance record is created. |
| Accessibility and UX | Existing semantic controls, visible labels, focusable buttons, native selects, responsive cards, disabled-state treatment, and evidence disclosures were retained. Desktop and mobile visual hierarchy was reviewed. |

Validation completed successfully with Prettier, `pnpm exec tsc --noEmit`, and the production build. The build continues to emit the existing non-blocking large-chunk advisory. Desktop evidence was captured at 1440×1000 and mobile evidence at 390×844. Visual review confirms preserved local campaign behavior, explicit unavailable/unconfigured/blocked states, disabled consequential actions, and absence of fabricated outcomes.

Production activation would require consent-compliant recipient data, governed templates and sender identity, provider integration, quiet-hour and opt-out enforcement, privacy and safeguarding review, rate limits, idempotency, delivery callbacks, secure audit logging, abuse prevention, incident response, and accountable domain review for transactional, marketing, education, community, security, financial, regulated, or user-impact claims. No SMS send, delivery, marketing, cost, compliance, or business outcome is claimed here.
