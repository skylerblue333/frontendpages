# CrossChainInterop review

The former route used a shared unavailable notice describing fabricated chain support, TVL, bridge timing, fees, completed transfers, pending transfers, and a bridge execution control. It has been replaced with a strictly typed, accessible, local-only interoperability-readiness workspace.

The new screen states that no networks, bridge contracts, token mapping, quote, balance, wallet, signature, transfer, destination receipt, or saved movement is loaded or persisted. Route loading, quote retrieval, transfer review, and bridging controls are disabled. The route documents networks and chain IDs, bridge contracts, token mappings, deployments, validators, routes, security review, assets, amounts, balances, allowances, limits, fees, quote expiry, slippage, wallet scope, signer authorization, message IDs, nonces, signatures, relays, source confirmations, destination transactions, finality, reorgs, duplicate prevention, failure, refunds, timeouts, retries, pauses, incident response, custody, monitoring, and audit. Its search field filters static capability notes only and never queries chains, loads routes, calculates quotes, accesses wallets, signs messages, relays transfers, or persists a receipt.

`prettier`, `tsc --noEmit`, and the production build completed successfully. Desktop evidence shows balanced bridge-state/release cards and a four-item capability map. Mobile evidence stacks the same content cleanly at 390×844 without clipping; chain identity, quote, authorization, transfer lifecycle, reorg, recovery, custody, audit, and unavailable-action disclosures remain readable.
