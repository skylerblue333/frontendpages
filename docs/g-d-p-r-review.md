# GDPR review

The `/g-d-p-r` route was upgraded from an authenticated CRUD shell into a truthful **privacy and data-rights readiness workspace**. It does not claim that personal data, consent, legal bases, data-rights requests, privacy settings, audit results, certifications, or regulatory status exist.

| Area | Result |
|---|---|
| Identity, legal basis, and data inventory | No data-subject identity, processing purpose, legal basis, controller or processor scope, data inventory, retention rule, or consent record is loaded. |
| Access, export, correction, and deletion | No verified request, identity-proofing flow, access bundle, export format, correction workflow, deletion policy, exception, or completion evidence is connected. |
| Privacy, security, and processor controls | No privacy setting, access policy, encryption state, processor contract, transfer assessment, breach workflow, audit event, or incident evidence exists. |
| Complaints, review, and accountability | No supervisory contact, complaint record, response deadline, accountable owner, legal review, independent assessment, or compliance claim is available. |
| Interaction boundary | Search filters immutable local notes; management buttons update only an `aria-live` unavailable status. No personal-data or privacy mutation exists. |
| Accessibility | Semantic landmarks, labelled search, keyboard-safe controls, decorative icon hiding, and live status feedback are included. |

The implementation was formatted with Prettier, passed `pnpm exec tsc --noEmit`, and passed the production build. The existing large-chunk advisory remains non-blocking. Desktop evidence was captured at 1440×1000 and mobile evidence at 390×844. Visual review confirms the service boundary, no-personal-data/no-rights-workflow/no-compliance-claim disclosures, and responsive readiness map are readable without fabricated privacy or legal claims.

Production activation requires a data inventory, documented purposes and legal bases, data-subject authentication, request SLAs, deletion and retention controls, processor governance, transfer safeguards, incident response, accountable owners, legal review, and independent evidence. This screen does not determine whether any legal requirement is satisfied.
